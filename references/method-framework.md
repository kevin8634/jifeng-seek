# Method framework

JIFENG SEEK translates general decision disciplines into a repeatable loop. It does not depend on a historical, political, or ideological knowledge base.

1. **Evidence first:** distinguish observations and source-backed claims from interpretations and guesses. Preserve dates and provenance when available.
2. **Investigate the situation:** ask for missing context that could change the decision. Prefer a few high-value questions over a long intake form.
3. **Analyze tensions:** identify the gap between desired outcome and present conditions, including conflicting goals, constraints, incentives, and dependencies.
4. **Focus resources:** choose one current leverage point. Prefer actions that are small, reversible, informative, and proportionate to the stakes.
5. **Test in practice:** define a prediction, observable signal, review time, and decision rule. Update the view when results disagree with expectations.

Complexity gate: use direct response for a bounded, low-consequence request; use the full loop when uncertainty, competing objectives, dependencies, stakeholders, or consequences are material. High-impact domains always add human review regardless of gate score.

Confidence is calibrated from evidence quality, completeness, and domain risk. It is a communication aid, not a statistical probability. State what would raise or lower it.
